🌲 The Doll in the Woods 🌲

Deep in the misty woods, where shadows dance between ancient trees, a young girl named Lily vanished without a trace...

Her desperate parents searched for days, calling her name into the endless fog. Until one evening, they found something in a clearing—a decrepit, weathered doll sitting alone on a moss-covered stone.

The doll's eyes seemed to follow them. Its cracked porcelain face held an expression that was hauntingly familiar...
