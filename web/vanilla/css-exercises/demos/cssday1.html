The Majestic Tiger

The largest of all Asian big cats, tigers rely on sight and sound rather than smell for hunting. They are solitary predators, capable of consuming over 80 pounds of meat in a single meal. On average, female tigers give birth to 2–4 cubs every two years. Tigers are known for their strength, agility, and iconic striped fur.

Key Characteristics

  • Powerful Build: Muscular bodies designed for strength and stealth.
  • Distinctive Stripes: Each tiger has a unique stripe pattern, like human fingerprints.
  • Solitary Hunters: Prefer to live and hunt alone.
  • Excellent Swimmers: Unlike most cats, tigers enjoy swimming.
  • Territorial: Mark large territories with scent and scratch marks.
