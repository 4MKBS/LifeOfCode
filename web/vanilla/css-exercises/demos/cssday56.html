04 Scroll Typography

The scroll becomes a canvas for temporal typography.

As the user scrolls, each letter materializes from the void, creating a choreographed dance of revelation. The pace of discovery is in the reader's hands, turning passive viewing into an active, meditative experience.

This demo showcases scroll-driven text reveal effects built with vanilla CSS and JavaScript. Each section uses intersection observers and scroll position calculations to progressively reveal characters and words as you navigate through the page.
